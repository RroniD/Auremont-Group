About the company

Founded 1990 · Peja, Kosovo

Auremont is a construction company based in Peja, founded in 1990. We produce ready-mix concrete and rebar mesh, build infrastructure and buildings, and provide architectural design. Keeping these processes in-house lets us control quality and schedules at every stage of the work.

What we do 01 / 07

We make it ourselves. We build it ourselves.

Seven lines of work under one company, from raw material to handover.

  1. 01Infrastructure

    Water supply and sewerage

    Drinking water networks and sewer systems for towns and villages. Our own crews handle excavation, pipe laying, manholes and connections to the existing network.

  2. 02Infrastructure

    Irrigation channels

    Construction and rehabilitation of irrigation channels with concrete lining, along with the gates and structures that distribute water across the fields.

  3. 03Infrastructure

    Dams and reservoirs

    Dams, weirs and water reservoirs built to the hydraulic design, with concrete and waterproofing checked at every stage.

  4. 04Production

    Concrete production

    Ready-mix concrete from our own plant, with controlled mix designs and strength classes to project specification, delivered by truck mixer straight to site.

  5. 05Production

    Rebar production

    Cutting, bending and welding of rebar and mesh to the project schedules, ready to install on site.

  6. 06Buildings

    Construction projects

    Residential, commercial and public buildings, from foundations to turnkey handover.

  7. 07Architecture

    Architecture

    Architectural design and technical documentation for permits and construction, from a team that also knows the site.
