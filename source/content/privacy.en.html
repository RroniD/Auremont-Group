This page explains what data auremont-group.com collects, why, how long it is kept and what rights you have. You can use the whole site without accepting cookies.

Who is responsible

Auremont Group, based in Peja, Kosovo, is responsible for the data collected on this site. For any question about personal data, write to info@auremont-group.com.

What we collect

When you contact us

The contact form does not store anything on the site. It opens your email app with the message filled in, and we only receive the email you choose to send: your name, phone or email address and the project description. We receive the same details when you write or call us directly. We use them to reply, to prepare an offer and, if we work together, for the contract.

Statistics, only with your consent

If you accept statistics, Google Analytics collects data about your visit: the pages and sections you read, the time of the visit, your type of device and browser, your approximate city or country and the page you came from. Google Analytics does not store your full IP address. We do not use this data for advertising and do not link it to your name.

Google map, only with your consent

The interactive map in the contact section is loaded from Google Maps. Until you allow it, you only see a map image served by our own site. When the map loads, Google receives your IP address and may set cookies under its own privacy policy.

Server logs

Like any website, our hosting provider's server automatically records the IP address, time of the request, the page requested and the browser type. These logs are used only to keep the site secure and working, and are deleted after a short period.

Legal basis

We process data in line with Kosovo's Law No. 06/L-082 on the Protection of Personal Data:

  • to answer your request and take steps before a contract;
  • with your consent, for statistics and the Google map;
  • in our legitimate interest, to keep the site secure;
  • to meet legal obligations, such as keeping contract and invoice records.

Who we share data with

We do not sell data or pass it on for marketing. We only share it with the providers that help us run the site and our communication: our hosting provider, our email provider and Google (Analytics and Maps, only with your consent). Google may process data outside Kosovo, including in the United States, under the safeguards set out in its data processing terms.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and correspondence: up to 24 months after the last contact, unless the law requires longer.
  • Statistics in Google Analytics: 14 months.
  • Your cookie choice: 12 months, after which we ask again.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict its use, receive it in a usable format and object to its processing. You can withdraw your consent at any time; this does not affect processing that happened before. Write to info@auremont-group.com and we will reply within 30 days.

If you think your data is not being handled properly, you have the right to complain to Kosovo's Information and Privacy Agency (AIP).

Security

The site is only served over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Access to emails and project documents is limited to the people who work on them.

Changes

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page. For important changes we ask for your cookie consent again.
